Accuracy & synchronization

This version uses the iPhone's system clock as its current UTC timestamp and calculates elapsed SI time from the epoch. It is a local-device chronometer, not a synchronized Galactic Reference Frame. Device clock accuracy, leap-second handling, and relativistic corrections are not independently supplied by this prototype. Reopen the app to refresh after iOS suspends it.
